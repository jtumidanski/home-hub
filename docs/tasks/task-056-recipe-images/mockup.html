Shakshuka

Eggs poached in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce. Great for brunch.

4 servings 10m prep + 25m cook ↗ Source
BreakfastVegetarianOne Pan

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Feta, to serve

Instructions

  1. Sauté the onion and pepper in olive oil until soft, about 8 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, cumin and paprika; cook 1 minute.
  3. Pour in tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes until thickened.
  4. Make 6 wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 6–8 minutes.
  5. Top with feta and herbs. Serve with crusty bread.

The browser never talks to MinIO. Image bytes are streamed through recipe-service, so the existing cookie auth and tenant/household scoping protect them and no presigned URLs are needed. If MinIO is down, only image features degrade (503). Recipes, the planner and shopping keep working.

v is the image id, which is new on every replace. That lets the browser cache images "forever" while a new photo still shows up immediately.

What happens on upload

  1. Recipe looked up within the caller's tenant + household (else 404).
  2. DecodeConfig sniffs the real type and dimensions. Rejected if not JPEG/PNG/WebP (415) or > 40 MP (422), before the full decode.
  3. Decode, apply the EXIF orientation, composite transparency onto white.
  4. Resize to full (longest edge ≤ 1600px) and thumb (≤ 480px) and re-encode as JPEG q82. All EXIF/GPS metadata is dropped.
  5. Put both objects at {tenant}/{household}/recipes/{recipeId}/{imageId}/….
  6. Upsert the recipe_images row (one per recipe) in a transaction.
  7. Delete the previous image's objects (best effort, logged).

Out of scope (v1)

  • Multiple images / galleries, step photos
  • Add by URL, auto-import from source URL (og:image), clipboard paste
  • In-app cropping or rotation
  • Images in cook mode, the meal planner or the dashboard
  • HEIC/AVIF input; WebP output
